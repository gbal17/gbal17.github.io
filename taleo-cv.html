Employment History

Presented in reverse-chronological order, following the structure of my official FAO personnel record. Full role descriptions and source links are preserved as documented.

Land Degradation Neutrality

Apr 2026 – Present

NSLD, Land and Water Division

Serving as Geospatial Analyst under FAO NSLD (GCP/GLO/11848/GFF), supporting 21 UNCCD country Parties in SDG Indicator 15.3.1 / Land Degradation Neutrality reporting for the 2026 PRAIS4 cycle (COP17 deadline).

Designed and implemented a modular GEE-to-PRAIS4 Python pipeline covering all four SO1 sub-indicators (Land Cover, Land Productivity Dynamics, Soil Organic Carbon, SDG 15.3.1 composite), generating PRAIS4-ready tabular and raster outputs directly from Google Earth Engine exports — replacing a manual, non-standardized process with a repeatable one applicable across all 21 country contexts.

Piloted the full workflow for Mongolia, validating its transferability ahead of rollout to the remaining countries in the 2026 reporting cycle.

Led migration of GEE scripts and assets from third-party vendor infrastructure into FAO-controlled infrastructure (corporate GEE project, GitHub repository), removing external dependency and giving FAO direct ownership of the toolchain. Provided hands-on country-level support for PRAIS4 data upload, technical documentation, and knowledge transfer.

Supervisor: Livia Peiser, Senior Land and Water Officer

Monitoring Resilience — Afghanistan

Feb – Apr 2026

FAO Afghanistan, in collaboration with UNOSAT/UNITAR

Served as Geospatial Analyst under FAO Afghanistan, in collaboration with UNOSAT/UNITAR, supporting the AWARE platform (Afghanistan Watch for Agriculture, Resilience and Risk Exposure; data.apps.fao.org/aware) — a geospatial decision-support system covering ~401 sub-national districts with 60+ indicators across six thematic pillars.

Supervised design and computation of the district-level INFORM Risk Index, structured across three pillars (Hazard & Exposure, Vulnerability, Lack of Coping Capacity) in a Pillar → Category → Sub-component → Indicator hierarchy. Led systematic QA of the underlying calculation workbook against the AFG Final Narrative Report 2026 (UNOSAT/UNITAR v1.0), producing a traceability document that established a verifiable audit trail for the index's methodology.

Prepared and published 35 composite INFORM layers to the CKAN catalogue in compliance with FAO interoperability standards. Developed stakeholder presentations and capacity-development materials supporting platform dissemination among FAO Afghanistan colleagues and national partners.

Supervisor: Matieu Henry, Technical Officer, NSL (Land and Water Division)

Land Cover — Syria

Nov – Dec 2025

FAO Syria

Designed and implemented an automated national land cover production workflow in Google Earth Engine and Python, integrating AlphaEarth Foundations Satellite Embeddings V1, Sentinel-2 imagery, and Random Forest classification to produce a 10m-resolution national land cover map for drought impact assessment (openknowledge.fao.org).

Built and harmonized training/validation datasets using Collect Earth Online, applying FAO geospatial standards (LCML/ISO 19144-2) throughout classification and accuracy assessment. Delivered national land cover, cropland (irrigated/rainfed), and grazing-land datasets that directly support Syria's agricultural monitoring and drought decision-making. Produced full technical documentation and metadata ensuring the product's interoperability and reuse.

Supervisor: Matieu Henry, Technical Officer, NSL (Land and Water Division)

Flood Impact — Pakistan

Oct – Nov 2025

FAO Pakistan

Provided technical assistance to FAO Pakistan for geospatial impact assessment of floods on cropland using GIS/RS tools (FAO news release). Processed Sentinel-1/2, Landsat, and VIIRS imagery for land and water monitoring, classification, and accuracy assessment in line with FAO LCML/ISO standards.

Updated 2023–2024 cropland and crop-type layers, generated flood extent and duration maps (KP, Punjab, Sindh), and assessed impacts by irrigation system and crop type (crop-type maps: KP, Punjab, Sindh).

Developed automated workflows in Google Earth Engine, harmonized spatial datasets, and uploaded the newly developed layers to the FAO Agro-Informatics Platform — directly informing FAO's national flood response. Supported capacity development materials, technical documentation, and presentations for FAO and national partners, including province-level one-pagers (KP, Punjab, Sindh) and the full published report.

Supervisor: Matieu Henry, Technical Officer, NSL (Land and Water Division)

University of Lisbon

Mar 2020 – Jul 2023

Post-Doctoral Researcher · School of Agriculture, University of Lisbon · Lisbon, Portugal

Estimated shrub live fuel moisture content across mainland Portugal using GIS/RS techniques and satellite-derived indicators, contributing to a near-real-time operational LFMC product supporting national decision-making (MDPI, 2025). Mapped national fire regimes using Landsat imagery and time-series analysis, published as "Regimes de fogo em Portugal Continental" and "Cartografia de Regimes de Fogo à Escala da Freguesia (1980–2017)", and analyzed traditional extensive pastoralism and its dependence on fire through spatial and temporal GIS/RS assessments.

Developed and automated monitoring of the national fuel break network using Sentinel-2, Google Earth Engine, and Python, validated against GEDI data (Springer, 2022). Applied image classification, validation, and accuracy assessment following international and FAO geospatial standards throughout. Extended this fire-regime methodology to Turkey using MODIS fire observations.

Undertaken concurrently with an EU Aid Volunteers deployment to SRD Vietnam (Jan 2021 – Nov 2022, below).

Supervisor: José M.C. Pereira, Full Professor

Istanbul Technical University

Oct 2012 – May 2018

Post-Doctoral Researcher · Eurasia Institute of Earth Science, Istanbul Technical University · Istanbul, Türkiye

Worked as a post-doctoral researcher at the Eurasia Institute of Earth Science, on the KAMAG–TÜBİTAK project to develop an integrated emission inventory over Turkey. Conducted GIS/RS-based atmospheric and land monitoring analyses using Python and satellite data from geostationary and polar-orbiting sensors. Produced a multi-temporal satellite analysis of biomass burning over Turkey and contributed to the design and implementation of the national Fire Information System of Turkey (UYANBIL) and the Biomass Burning Eurasia platform, ensuring data standardization, validation, and alignment with international geospatial best practices.
